Global News TV – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Global News TV ("the app") lists live news streams that news channels publish on YouTube and plays them in an embedded YouTube player. This page explains what information the app and the third-party services built into it handle.

Who handles what

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no backend server of its own, and the developer does not receive your personal information. However, third-party services that the app uses or connects to may process information about you independently, under their own policies: YouTube and Google (video playback, advertising, consent management), Firebase (analytics and crash reports, a Google service) and GitHub (which hosts the stream list). Each is described below.

What is stored on your device

Your favorite channels, filter choices (country, language, category) and theme choice are stored locally on your device. They are not intentionally transmitted by the app. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in its own backups (for example Android's backup to your Google account or iOS device backups). These settings are removed from the app's local storage when you uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and restore features.

The stream list (GitHub Pages)

The app downloads a public list of live streams, a JSON file hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, this lets GitHub, and the infrastructure providers it uses to deliver content, see your IP address and basic request details. The app sends nothing else with the request. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The list is compiled by an automated script that uses YouTube API Services to read public information about live streams of news channels (titles, thumbnails, viewer counts). That script does not receive or process any information about the people who use the app.

YouTube

Videos are played by the YouTube embedded player, which is part of YouTube API Services. YouTube content is provided by YouTube and the channels that publish it, and YouTube (Google) may collect information about your viewing in accordance with its own policies. Your use of YouTube features and content in the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and by using them you agree to be bound by those terms. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners, not the app's developer, may process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to deliver and personalize ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and for other purposes described in Google's policies. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising policy.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. The app does not initialize the ads SDK or request ads until that consent flow reports that ads may be requested, either because you gave consent or because consent is not required where you are. The consent flow itself contacts Google to determine whether a consent message applies to you. Where the message is shown (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you can review or change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Analytics (Firebase)

The app includes Firebase Analytics (a Google service). It is switched off when the app starts and is turned on only after the same consent flow reports that data may be used; if you withdraw consent it is turned off again.

  • Collected automatically by Firebase: such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, app usage events (for example app opens and screen views) and a Firebase app-instance identifier.
  • Sent by the app itself: nothing. The app does not send custom analytics events, user identifiers or account information, and it does not collect crash reports.

See Firebase privacy information.

Platform-specific notes

  • Android: ads may use your Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device's settings. The app's store listing contains the data-safety information required by Google Play.
  • iOS: after the consent step, the app may show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads can still appear but without access to the advertising identifier for tracking. The app's store listing contains the privacy information required by Apple.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Your choices

  • Review or change ad and data consent in Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown where it applies).
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its locally stored settings (see the note on backups above).

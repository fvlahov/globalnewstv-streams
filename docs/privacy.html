Global News TV – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Global News TV ("the app") lists live news streams that channels publish on YouTube and plays them in an embedded YouTube player. This page explains what data the app handles. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own.

What stays on your device

Your favorite channels, your filter choices (country, language, category) and your theme choice are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

The stream list

The app downloads a public list of streams (a JSON file hosted on GitHub Pages). Like any web request, this lets the host see your IP address. The app does not send any other information with the request.

YouTube

Videos are played by the YouTube embedded player. YouTube (Google) may collect data about your viewing according to its own terms. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app uses YouTube API Services.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use your device's advertising identifier and other device information to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy settings. On iOS the app also asks for permission to track you before using the advertising identifier. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. These services receive technical data such as device model, operating system version, app version, an app-instance identifier and crash traces. See Firebase privacy information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown where consent applies).
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

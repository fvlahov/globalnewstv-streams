Global News TV – Support

Help for the Global News TV app on iPhone and Android

Need help, found a problem or want a channel added?
Email franko.vlahov@gmail.com and include your device (for example "iPhone 15, iOS 18"), and, if a stream is involved, the channel's name. I reply as soon as I can.

A stream does not play

  • Some channels do not allow their live stream to be embedded in other apps, and a channel may be off air at the moment. Try another channel.
  • Pull the list down to refresh. The list of streams is updated about every 30 minutes.
  • Check your internet connection. Without one, the app can show the last list it loaded (up to 3 days old) but cannot play video.
  • If it keeps happening for a particular channel, email me the channel's name.

The list is empty or old

The app shows "Offline: showing saved streams" when it cannot reach the server and is using the last list it saved. Reconnect and pull down to refresh.

Ads

  • The app is free and is supported by ads. Ads never appear on top of or inside the video.
  • Ad-free for one hour: open Settings → Ads → Watch video. After you watch a short video, ads are switched off for an hour.
  • Change your ad choices: Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown where a consent choice applies, for example in Europe). On iPhone you can also change tracking permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Missing a news channel

Email me the channel's name and the link to its YouTube channel. I will take a look; channels need to broadcast live news and allow their stream to be embedded.

My favorites or settings disappeared

Favorites, filters and your theme choice are stored only on your device. They are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app has no accounts, so they cannot be restored from a server.

Your data and privacy

There are no accounts, and the app does not collect data for its developer. Read the full Privacy Policy.

About the content

The live streams are published by the news channels themselves on YouTube and are played with the YouTube player; their content belongs to them.
